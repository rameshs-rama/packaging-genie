Built-in self-test

Runs the default SKUs individually on the Europe and Industrial pallets, the mixed-pallet tests A–D (plus extra exact-quantity, multi-pallet and container checks). Every scenario is checked for footprint, overlap, height, weight, orientation, support, carton counts and recalculated percentages. The tests always use the built-in default data, not your edited master data.
